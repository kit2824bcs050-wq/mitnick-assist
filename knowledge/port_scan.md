PORT SCAN INCIDENT GUIDANCE

Indicators:
- Large number of destination ports
- High connection rate
- Short-lived connections
- Reconnaissance activity

SOC Investigation:
- Identify the scanning source
- Review targeted ports
- Determine whether exposed services exist
- Check firewall logs
- Look for exploitation attempts following reconnaissance

Response:
- Restrict suspicious sources
- Harden unnecessary services
- Review firewall rules
- Continue monitoring the source
