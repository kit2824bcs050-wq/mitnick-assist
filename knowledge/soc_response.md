GENERAL SOC INCIDENT RESPONSE

1. Validate the alert.
2. Collect supporting evidence.
3. Determine affected assets.
4. Assess incident severity.
5. Investigate source and timeline.
6. Contain confirmed malicious activity.
7. Preserve relevant logs and evidence.
8. Recover affected systems.
9. Document the incident.
10. Continue monitoring for recurrence.

High-impact containment actions should require analyst approval.
